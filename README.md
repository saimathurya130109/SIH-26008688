SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

### **1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution**

We propose developing **Farm2Market**, an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) with consumers, retailers, supermarkets and wholesale buyers. The platform aims to reduce the involvement of unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

Farmers can register on the platform and upload details of their agricultural products, including crop name, quantity, quality, location and expected price. Buyers can browse the available products, compare prices and place orders directly with farmers.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) to predict the demand for agricultural products based on previous sales, seasonal trends and market requirements. This helps farmers make better decisions about crop production and avoid excess production.

A smart logistics system helps connect farmers with delivery partners and identifies efficient transportation routes. The platform also provides order tracking and digital payment facilities to ensure transparent transactions.

The application will support regional languages such as Tamil and English, making it easier for rural farmers to access and use the platform.

### **2. How It Addresses the Problem**

The existing agricultural supply chain involves multiple intermediaries between farmers and consumers. This increases product prices for consumers while reducing the profits received by farmers.

Our proposed solution addresses these problems in the following ways:

- **Eliminates Unnecessary Middlemen:** Directly connects farmers with consumers and bulk buyers, reducing dependence on multiple intermediaries.
- **Improves Farmers' Income:** Farmers can display their products and expected prices, helping them reach more buyers and receive better returns.
- **Reduces Consumer Prices:** Direct purchasing reduces additional costs added by multiple intermediaries.
- **Reduces Transportation Costs:** Smart logistics and route optimization help identify efficient delivery routes.
- **Prevents Food Wastage:** AI-based demand forecasting helps estimate market requirements and reduce excess supply.
- **Ensures Transparency:** Digital transactions, product details and order tracking improve trust between farmers and buyers.

### **3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution**

The uniqueness of Farm2Market is that it combines direct agricultural marketing with AI-based demand forecasting and smart logistics in a single digital platform.

Its innovative features include:

1. **AI-Based Demand Forecasting:** Uses previous sales data, seasonal patterns and market trends to predict future demand for agricultural products.
2. **Smart Price Comparison:** Helps farmers and buyers compare available product prices and make informed purchasing decisions.
3. **Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Connection:** Provides a common platform for farmers, FPOs, individual consumers and bulk buyers without unnecessary intermediaries.
4. **Intelligent Route Optimization:** Suggests efficient delivery routes to reduce transportation time and costs.
5. **Multilingual Support:** Provides Tamil and English language options to make the platform accessible to rural farmers.
6. **Real-Time Order Tracking:** Allows buyers to monitor their orders and provides transparency throughout the delivery process.

**Overall, Farm2Market provides a smart, transparent and efficient agricultural marketplace that aims to improve farmers' earnings, make agricultural products affordable for consumers and reduce supply chain inefficiencies.**

## **2. Methodology and Process for Implementation**

The project will be developed through the following steps:

**Step 1: User Registration**

Farmers, consumers and wholesale buyers can create accounts by entering their basic details.

**Step 2: Product Uploading**

Farmers can upload their crop details, product images, quantity and expected price.

**Step 3: Product Searching**

Consumers and wholesale buyers can search for available products, compare prices and place orders directly.

**Step 4: AI-Based Demand Prediction**

AI will analyze previous sales and seasonal trends to predict the demand for agricultural products. This helps farmers plan their production.

**Step 5: Price Comparison**

The platform helps farmers and buyers compare product prices to make better purchasing and selling decisions.

**Step 6: Smart Delivery System**

Google Maps will help identify delivery locations and suggest suitable routes to reduce transportation time and costs.

**Step 7: Order and Payment**

Buyers can place orders and make digital payments. Farmers and buyers can also check their order status.

**Step 8: Testing and Implementation**

The website will be tested to check whether all features work correctly. After testing, it will be made available for users.

## Feasibility and Viability

### **1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea**

Farm2Market is a practical and useful idea that can be developed using available technologies. It connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers through a simple digital platform.

- **Technical Feasibility:** The website can be developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and MySQL. AI and Google Maps can be integrated to support demand prediction and delivery.
- **Economic Feasibility:** The project can be developed with a low initial investment using free and open-source software.
- **Operational Feasibility:** Farmers and buyers can easily use the platform through smartphones or computers.
- **Social Feasibility:** It supports farmers by providing better selling opportunities and helps consumers purchase fresh products at reasonable prices.

Therefore, Farm2Market is technically possible, affordable to develop and useful for society.

### **2. Potential Challenges and Risks**

While developing and using Farm2Market, we may face some challenges.

1. **Lack of Technical Knowledge:** Some farmers may not know how to use digital platforms.
2. **Internet Connectivity:** Farmers in rural areas may have poor internet access.
3. **Transportation Problems:** Delivering agricultural products on time may be difficult in some areas.
4. **Product Quality:** Maintaining the freshness and quality of agricultural products during transportation can be challenging.
5. **Price Fluctuations:** Agricultural product prices may change frequently depending on market demand and supply.
6. **Data Security:** Protecting farmers' and buyers' personal information and payment details is important.

### **3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges**

The following solutions can help overcome the challenges.

1. **Simple User Interface:** Develop an easy-to-use application with clear instructions and regional language support such as Tamil and English.
2. **Low Internet Support:** Make the platform lightweight and provide basic features that work with slow internet connections.
3. **Better Logistics:** Connect farmers with local delivery partners to make transportation easier.
4. **Quality Management:** Provide product details, images and quality information before placing orders.
5. **Regular Price Updates:** Update market prices regularly to help farmers and buyers make better decisions.
6. **Secure Platform:** Use secure login systems and trusted payment gateways to protect user information and transactions.


Farm2Market is a feasible and valuable solution to reduce the problems caused by multiple intermediaries in agriculture. Although challenges such as internet connectivity, transportation and technical knowledge may arise, they can be managed through simple technology and proper planning. The platform can improve farmers' earnings, reduce consumer prices and create a more efficient agricultural supply chain.
